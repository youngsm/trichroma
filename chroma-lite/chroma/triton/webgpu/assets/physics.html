Model scope
Three synthetic demonstrations using the native simulation’s optical tables, Philox random streams, polarized Fresnel boundaries, polarized Rayleigh scattering, and effective fluorescent surface spectrum and delay. WGSL arithmetic is float32; validation records its differences from the CPU float64 intersection reference. This is not a general detector event backend. Time gating reveals completed flights without resimulating. UV is violet false color; path brightness is for visibility, not radiance. Browser GPU hardware and power settings determine performance.
